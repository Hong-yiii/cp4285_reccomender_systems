# Dormancy and target share, 3 November 2026

Block-2 dormancy contrast and a target-share sweep, three seeds per cell. Results and explanations: [CONTINUED-TRAINING.md](../CONTINUED-TRAINING.md#dormancy-and-target-share-3-november-2026).

## Dormant accounts

- 100 matched accounts, 3% event budget, block-2 attack.
- Instruments: dormant −2.59 against continuing −2.81.
- Video Games: dormant −3.84 against continuing −3.01.
- CDs: dormant −6.69 against continuing −6.49.
- No consistent dormant advantage. Plausible explanation: block-0 exposure still reaches these users, and they return with real histories, so a one-block gap is not a reset. This supports “existing accounts are enough,” not “dormant accounts are easy.”
- The contrast is confounded with group composition (dormant/continuing pools differ in size and history), and block-2 effects are not directly comparable to block-1.

## Target share

- Bandwagon at 25/50/75% of profile events, fixed 3% dose.
- Instruments −1.79/−1.63/−1.75; Video Games −1.33/−2.18/−1.62; CDs −2.74/−4.00/−3.61.
- Not monotone: 50% is usually as good or better than 75%.
- Plausible explanation: target-event redundancy. Once about half the profile pushes the target, repeats add little, and 75% loses filler diversity. Bandwagon AUC stays 1.00, so lower target share buys no stealth here.
- A clone target-share sweep is still the clean test of target association versus realism.
